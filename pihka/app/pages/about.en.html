About this dataset

This is a demonstration deployment of Pihka published by the Ghent Centre for Digital Humanities. It presents a small sample of literary authors and their works so you can try out the platform's tables, curated perspectives, map view, faceted filtering and full-text search.

Full texts are provided by Project Gutenberg. Pihka means resin in Finnish: the name reflects the idea of preserving a database in resin.

The underlying data is illustrative and assembled for demonstration only. For the platform itself and how to publish your own dataset, see the instructions.
